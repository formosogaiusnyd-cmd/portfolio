Enterprise · Finance

From 0 to 1.08M Clicks: Scaling Organic for a National Finance Platform

A national financial services platform had the domain authority but no content architecture to convert it. The diagnosis: fragmented topical coverage, Core Web Vitals failures on high-traffic pages, and zero structured data across a YMYL category demanding E-E-A-T proof.

The fix was systematic. I built a topical cluster map that revealed 200+ content gaps, ran a CWV audit that surfaced LCP failures above 4s on 40% of indexed pages, and stood up structured data (Article, FAQ, BreadcrumbList) across key verticals. A refresh cadence kept high-traffic articles current and authoritative. Twelve months later, the platform had moved from near-zero organic presence to over a million clicks.

E-Commerce · Furniture

$1M Revenue in 28 Days: Furniture E-commerce at Scale

A furniture e-commerce brand was competing in a high-difficulty space with a site architecture working against it. Widespread keyword cannibalization meant category and product pages were fighting each other instead of complementing each other. Commercial-intent queries were landing on informational pages.

I restructured the content silo, resolving cannibalization through targeted page consolidations and redirect management, and realigned product and category page optimization to match transactional intent. The results — tracked and confirmed in GA4 — came in at 246K active users and over $1M revenue within a single 28-day window, representing 797.5% YoY user growth.

Healthcare · Dental Practice

41.2K Clicks in 12 Months: Healthcare SEO in a YMYL Space

Healthcare SEO carries a higher bar: Google's YMYL classification means E-E-A-T isn't optional, it's the price of entry. This dental practice needed local visibility and the kind of content authority that earns it.

I implemented LocalBusiness schema to establish entity clarity, optimized the Google Business Profile for local intent queries, and built a hub-and-spoke content model mapping key dental procedures to patient concerns. Note: the 0.5% CTR reflects the informational nature of most queries in this space — SERP features (knowledge panels, local packs) absorb clicks before organic listings. Impressions growth and position improvement were the meaningful signals here.
